On Wharf St

Sayu sits on Wharf St, in the middle of Murwillumbah's small Art Deco river-town dining strip, the same stretch as Wild Thyme Dining. Chef Geoff Anstee runs the kitchen for 50 seats, alfresco tables included, with a full bar behind it. The restaurant is a current nominee in AGFG's Readers' Choice 2026 awards, Japanese category, Northern Rivers region.
